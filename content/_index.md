I'm a Threat Researcher at Sysdig, focused on Linux and cloud security, threat detection, and attacker behavior.

My work sits at the intersection of:

- Threat research & emerging attacker TTPs
- Linux & cloud security
- Detection engineering

I'm exploring what happens when AI enters this loop, how it changes the economics of attacks, the way attackers operate, and how defenders detect them.

Earlier in my career, I worked with the European Space Agency on time-series anomaly detection for Delay Tolerant Networks.

If you're interested in how attacks evolve, and how defenders should respond, follow along.
